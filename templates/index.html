HOW IT WORKS

Four practical stages to cleaner water.

The system combines filtration and disinfection stages to improve water quality before use.

01

Solar Power

Solar panels capture sunlight and provide renewable electricity for the purification system.

02

Sediment Filtration

The first filter reduces suspended particles such as sand, dust and visible impurities.

03

Activated Carbon

Carbon filtration helps reduce unwanted taste, odor and selected organic contaminants.

04

UV Sterilization

UV treatment provides a final disinfection stage to help make treated water safer.
